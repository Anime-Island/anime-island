About Anime Island

Anime Island is a reality competition show hosted by Kenjaku, featuring 12 contestants from different anime universes.

The contestants arrive on the island to compete in challenges, meet new people, form friendships and rivalries, and fight for the title of Anime Island's winner.

What starts as an entertaining competition filled with comedy, arguments, and unexpected interactions slowly reveals that there's more to Anime Island than meets the eye.

The Competition

12 contestants enter Anime Island to compete in a series of challenges. Throughout the competition, contestants will face eliminations until only two remain.

The final two will compete for the title of Anime Island's winner.

The Island

Anime Island is where the entire competition takes place. The island serves as the contestants' home throughout the competition, featuring locations where they can interact, relax, compete, and spend time together.

The Host

Meet Kenjaku, the host of Anime Island!

Kenjaku is responsible for welcoming the contestants, introducing challenges, and keeping the competition moving.

He'll be guiding the contestants throughout their time on the island.
