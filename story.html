About Anime Island

Anime Island is a reality competition show hosted by Kenjaku. Twelve contestants from different anime universes arrive on the island to compete in challenges, meet new people, form friendships and rivalries, and fight for the title of Anime Island's winner.

What starts as an entertaining competition filled with comedy, arguments, and unexpected interactions slowly reveals that there's more to Anime Island than meets the eye.
